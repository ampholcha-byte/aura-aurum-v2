## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the regal, auspicious, and wealth-attracting spirit of premium gold trading and wealth accumulation, directly inspired by the heritage of Bangkok’s renowned Yaowarat gold district. It balances traditional luxury with contemporary fintech clarity. 

### Visual Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Auspicious Wealth & Prestige:** Radiant warm gold accents and regal ruby carmine highlights instill confidence, fortune, and security.
- **Warm & Approachable Luxury:** Stepping away from cold metallic minimalism, the system uses sun-drenched ivory, soft champagne satin sheens, and warm espresso typography to create an inviting, human-centered financial environment.
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** High legibility, crisp structural alignments, and tactile micro-elevations ensure financial figures, gold rates, and investment tiers feel rock-solid and institutional.

### Design Movement
- **Tactile Soft-Luxe (Warm Neomorphic-Editorial):** Multi-layered satin surfaces, warm ambient gold drop-shadows, subtle pillared borders, and glossy metallic badges create depth without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from physical 96.5% bullion gold, ceremonial Chinese-Thai imperial red ribbons, and smooth ivory parchment.

### Key Palettes & Roles
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / `#E5B826`):** Authentic Royal Gold. Used for primary badges, metallic gradient rims, key conversion buttons, ranking medallions, and radiant aura accents.
- **Secondary (`#A6192E` / `#B81D24`):** Imperial Ruby Crimson. Reserved for high-urgency callouts, auspicious ribbon banners, live buy/sell markers, notification badges, and strong conversion focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#F4E3A4` / `#FFF8E1`):** Pale Champagne Sheen. Used for surface tinting, highlights, gradient stops on card headers, and active state pill backings.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FCF9F2` & `#FFFFFF`):** Warm Ivory Parchment base background overlaid with pure crystalline white card surfaces to maintain extreme cleanliness and breathing room.
- **Neutral Ink (`#2C241E` / `#1A1412`):** Deep Roast Espresso. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks for all body copy and headlines, offering deep contrast while harmonizing warmly with gold accents.
- **Subtle Supporting Tints:**
  - Gold Border Glow: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.24)`
  - Shadow Umbra: `rgba(44, 36, 30, 0.06)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its crisp geometry, warm friendly terminal curves, and high legibility across numerals, financial figures, and Thai-Latin dual interfaces.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Rendered in Extra-Bold (`800`) or Bold (`700`) weights. For promotional anchors, headlines may receive a warm metallic radial gradient (`from #B8860B via #D4AF37 to #996515`) with a soft 1px downward warm text shadow for physical presence.
- **Auspicious Subtitles & Dates:** Encased in red pill chips or gold hairline ribbons using `label-lg` or `title-md` in semi-bold tracking.
- **Financial & Data Readouts:** Numbers for gold bar grams, baht rates, and rankings are displayed in tabular-numeric styling with heavier weights (`700`) to guarantee scanability and alignment across tables.
- **Body & Captions:** High-contrast espresso tone (`#2C241E`) set at generous line-heights (1.4–1.5x) to maintain effortless reading on light cream canvases.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a centered container, fluid-responsive 4-column (mobile), 8-column (tablet), and 12-column (desktop) grid.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Reserved for pairing gold medal badges with rank titles, avatar alignment, and icon-to-label distances.
- **Component Padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Cards, ranked tier list items, and modal dialogs consistently use `1rem` on mobile and expand to `1.5rem` on larger displays to create an uncrowded, premium feel.
- **Section Breaks (`space-xl`):** Substantial breathing intervals between promotional campaign headers, rank rosters, and call-to-action footers prevent visual noise.
- **Form Factors:** Mobile views maximize horizontal canvas width using condensed margins (`1rem`), while desktop views cap content max-width at `1140px` with expansive outer margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined warm-tinted lighting model that recreates gold's natural luminosity against ivory linen.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Parchment Base):** `#FCF9F2` flat background, layered with faint metallic wave ribbons (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
2. **Level 1 (Card & List Item Surface):** `#FFFFFF` with a dual-strata shadow:
   - Ambient: `0 4px 16px rgba(44, 36, 30, 0.04)`
   - Gold Luster Rim: `0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`
3. **Level 2 (Floating Action Buttons & Ranking Medallions):** 
   - Shadow: `0 6px 20px rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.22)`
   - Beveled stroke: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` inner inset for metallic tactility.
4. **Level 3 (Modals & Fixed Bottom Drawers):** 
   - `0 16px 40px rgba(44, 36, 30, 0.12)`, overlaid on a warm backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) bg-surface/80`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, friendly roundedness (`0.5rem` base, scaling to `1rem` for container cards and full `9999px` pills for interactive elements and badges).

### Shape Tokens
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem):** Input text boxes, small tooltip callouts, and segmented tabs.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Fortune ranking cards, transaction summaries, and gold asset display modules.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Ranking numbered badges, primary call-to-action buttons, date filter pills, and badge ribbons. This preserves the soft, friendly amulet-like feel seen in traditional gold medallions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Bullion Action:** Gradient fill `linear-gradient(135deg, #F3C343 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #B8860B 100%)`, text in `#FFFFFF` with subtle drop shadow, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 24px`. On hover, subtle bloom aura `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(229, 184, 38, 0.4)`.
- **Auspicious Crimson CTA:** `#A6192E` background, white text, pill-shaped. Used for urgent "Buy Now" or "Hot Trend" actions.
- **Secondary Champagne Button:** Border 1.5px solid `#D4AF37`, background `#FFFDF8`, text `#8A6715`.

### Cards & Ranked Tier Lists (Zodiac / Gold Holdings)
- **Structure:** Clean white card (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#EEDCA0`. 
- **List Item Layout:** Left-anchored 3D circular medal badge with number/zodiac crest, followed by bold headline and warm muted description (`#5E4F45`), right-anchored trend or profit value.
- **Hover/Active State:** Subtle inward scale and border shift to solid `#D4AF37`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Auspicious Ribbon Badge:** Crimson background (`#A6192E`), bold white text, flanked by rounded pill endings or folded banner tail styling.
- **Gold Tier Chip:** Pale gold surface (`#FFF9E6`), border `#D4AF37`, icon prefixed with circular bullion motif.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Warm ivory surface (`#FDFCFA`), border 1.5px `#E8D8BA`, text `#2C241E`. Focus state transitions border to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio with active state displaying a deep ruby red or burnished gold bullseye fill.

### Specialized Assets
- **Gold Ingot Price Tracker:** Container featuring a satin champagne gradient surface, stamped with authentic 96.5% gold bullion watermark, live tick indicator (green/crimson), and gold weight breakdown (`Baht` / `Gram`).
