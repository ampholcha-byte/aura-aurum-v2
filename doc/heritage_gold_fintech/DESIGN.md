## Brand & Style

The brand identity represents an exquisite synthesis of Yaowarat gold craftsmanship and progressive wealth-tech architecture. The character embodies four core pillars:
- **สง่างาม มีรากฐาน (Heritage):** Grounded in multi-generational goldsmith mastery, symbolized by auspicious deep royal wine and classic Chinese coin-bar geometrics.
- **น่าเชื่อถือ โปร่งใส (Trust & Transparency):** Real-time, transparent gold prices with clear 4-decimal weight accounting, evoking bulletproof institutional security.
- **ร่วมสมัย เข้าถึงง่าย (Modern Accessibility):** Intuitive mobile-first ergonomics, micro-savings flows, and frictionless wallet management.
- **หรูหราอย่างมีระดับ (Warm Prestige):** Tactile satin surfaces, champagne-gold metallic accents, and an ivory cream foundation rather than clinical sterile whites.

The visual style blends **Modern Luxury** with **Subtle Skeuomorphic / Tactile Accents**. It features luminous satin card backgrounds, hairline metallic borders, multi-layered depth for total asset portfolios, and gold-stamped tactile coin medals. The emotional experience balances the reverent warmth of entering a heritage Yaowarat flagship boutique with the speed and precision of high-frequency fintech.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from royal Thai-Chinese goldsmith heritage:

- **IVORY (`#F7F2E6`):** The primary canvas background and base card surface. It replaces cold digital white with warm, premium parchment tones.
- **BURGUNDY (`#7A0F1A`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for dominant portfolio hero banners, prestige navigation bars, primary trust insignias, and brand banners.
- **AUSPICIOUS RED (`#B31D1D`):** The operational red accent. Reserved for financial balance subtractions, urgent flash updates, promotional tags, and positive cultural callouts.
- **SOFT GOLD (`#D4AF37`):** The signature primary action tone. Powers primary conversion buttons, gold accumulation metrics, live spot price cards, and gold bullion representations.
- **CHAMPAGNE GOLD (`#C6A372`):** Used for micro-dividers, decorative ornamental crosses, input focus rings, secondary button outlines, and metallic borders.
- **DARK OLIVE BROWN (`#4B3E1F`) & DEEP ROAST (`#2A241A`):** Grounding neutral pigments used for primary typography, icons, and structured content hierarchy to eliminate harsh, artificial pure black (`#000000`).
- **FUNCTIONAL TINTS:**
  - `success`: `#2D7A4F` (Positive portfolio yield, verified KYC, active trading tags).
  - `surface-elevated`: `#FFFFFF` (Inner pure highlight surface for elevated sub-cards over Ivory background).
  - `gold-gradient`: `linear-gradient(135deg, #ECC86A 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #B89228 100%)`.
  - `burgundy-gradient`: `linear-gradient(135deg, #5A0811 0%, #7A0F1A 50%, #3D040A 100%)`.

## Typography

The type system blends authoritative prestige with clean fintech legibility.

1. **Display & Section Titles:** `Noto Serif` delivers regal craft, drawing direct ties to Yaowarat's goldsmith shopfronts and heritage signboards.
2. **Body, Micro-copy, & UI:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric precision, ensuring high legibility for mobile financial transactions, identity checks, and status badges.
3. **Thai Script Support:** Pairs harmoniously with system font stacks like `Noto Serif Thai` for classical headings and `Prompt` / `Sukhumvit Set` for UI body texts.
4. **Tabular Numerics Rule:** All prices (`฿48,750`), gold weight values (`0.0133 กรัม`), and yields must enforce `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'lnum' on` to align digits cleanly in ledger rows and fluctuating ticker cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a standard 8pt modular scale with a fluid, mobile-first grid container capped at 480px for handheld viewports and extending up to a max-width of 1280px for desktop web portals:

- **Mobile (Default, 360px - 430px):** Single-column stack with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and compact nested cards with `space-md: 1rem` internal padding. Quick-action icons run in a strict 4-column balanced cluster.
- **Tablet / Large Handhelds (431px - 768px):** 6-column fluid grid, expanding side gutters to `1.25rem`.
- **Desktop Dashboard (769px+):** 12-column grid displaying the gold price overview and total wealth portfolio side-by-side, flanked by gold accumulation ledger tables.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** Strict 44px minimum tap targets for transactional CTAs (`ฝากเงิน`, `ถอนเงิน`, `ขอรับทอง`), with persistent navigation docks accounting for 34px bottom home-indicator zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is executed through **Tonal Layers** combined with **Ambient Gold & Warm Umber Shadows**, avoiding standard cool-gray drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F7F2E6` ivory paper texture.
- **Level 1 (Subsurface Cards & Tickers):** White (`#FFFFFF`) or frosted translucent ivory with a delicate hairline border: `1px solid rgba(198, 163, 114, 0.25)` (Champagne Gold at 25% opacity) and a soft ambient glow: `0px 4px 20px rgba(75, 62, 31, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Prestige Hero Banners):** Deep Burgundy gradient (`linear-gradient(145deg, #7A0F1A 0%, #4D070F 100%)`) with an inner top rim glow (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) and diffused ground shadow: `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(122, 15, 26, 0.28)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Modals & Gold Coin Tokens):** Raised elements with 3D tactile embossing, dual-tone borders, and elevated soft shadow: `0px 14px 32px rgba(42, 36, 26, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system blends curved comfort with sleek geometry:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Employ `rounded-2xl` (16px to 20px) to produce an approachable, contemporary banking card feel.
- **Buttons, Status Pills, & Quick Action Badges:** Employ `rounded-full` (9999px) for friendly tactile pills.
- **Icon Pods:** Use squircle-rounded geometries (`rounded-xl`, 12px) for structured category grids.
- **Ornamental Accents:** Traditional Chinese geometric floral crosses and gold filigree dividers appear sparingly as hairline decorative vectors separating header sections.
